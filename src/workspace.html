Your workspace

Seven areas of the work, each holding the tools that support it. Open one, switch on what you need, and ignore the rest. Nothing is switched on without you asking for it.

Set up for · edit

How this works

Each area opens to show its tools. Switch one on and it appears right there, ready to use. Tools marked Not built are planned — switching them on shows what they will do, which is also how you tell us they matter. Everything you enter stays in this browser.

Something missing?

These came out of what agencies told us in interviews. If a repetitive, aggregate-data job is eating your week and it is not here, say so with the Feedback button.

About the workspace

Built by the NCAF AI Task Force, a student project at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, for the Community Action network. These are working drafts, not certified compliance software. Nothing here replaces your own review, and no tool on this page should be the last check before something reaches a funder.
