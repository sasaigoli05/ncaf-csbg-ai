Why this tool exists

Module 3 draws one hard line: aggregate data and your own words are fine, anything that identifies a person is not. This is that line, made checkable. It won't catch everything — in a small county, a detailed description can identify someone with no name attached at all — so treat a clean result as a good sign, not a guarantee.
